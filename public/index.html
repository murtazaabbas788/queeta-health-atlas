Study area & sources

Study area: Quetta District, Balochistan, Pakistan. The mapped facilities are concentrated in and around Quetta City. The current 49 points were checked against the supplied Quetta City tehsil boundary, whose source and date are being documented.

Facilities: Candidate locations were gathered from OpenStreetMap and from locations supplied by the project author. Selected attributes were checked against available public sources. Individual details and ownership remain under review.

Coordinates: A mapped point indicates an approximate location. Some points were derived from mapped polygons and do not necessarily mark entrances. Directions use the stored point.

Coverage: This is a developing academic dataset, not a complete register or a live account of services. Contact facilities directly to confirm details before travelling.
